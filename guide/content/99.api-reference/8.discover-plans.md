#### OPS-01 `GET /api/health` — CONFIRMED (decision 18: polled by uptime monitor)

Status: :badge[implemented]{color="success"}
- **Proof:** `tests/e2e/api.test.ts`
- Public. `200 { status: "ok", db: "ok", version: <git sha> }` / `503` with `db: "error"` (no error detail). Not cached. Used by uptime monitor and future Contabo/Docker healthcheck.

#### OPS-02 `POST /api/internal/jobs/notifications` · OPS-03 `POST /api/internal/jobs/purge` — CONFIRMED (new; internal)

Status: :badge[implemented]{color="success"} :badge[implemented]{color="success"}
- **Proof:** `tests/e2e/watch-parties.test.ts`
- Called by a scheduler with a `Bearer` secret (env `CRON_SECRET`, never in the guide); anything else → `404`. OPS-02 (every ~5–15 min) sends watch-party reminders and marks parties `live`/`ended`; OPS-03 (daily) purges accounts past their 30-day grace and expired evidence. Both idempotent. Scheduler: Vercel Cron or an external cron — Vercel's Hobby plan limits cron frequency, so this is checked against your plan at setup.

#### MED-01 `POST /api/uploads` — CONFIRMED (Cloudflare R2, decision 20.5) · returns a presigned upload URL; images ≤5 MB, voice ≤10 MB/≤3 min; evidence goes to a private bucket prefix served only via signed URLs.

Status: :badge[planned]{color="neutral"}
- Needed by drops (voice/image), avatars, talent images, application/claim evidence. Proposed: returns `{ mediaId, uploadUrl, expiresAt, maxBytes }` (pre-signed direct upload; the 4.5 MB Vercel function body limit rules out proxying uploads through Nitro). `purpose` enum (`avatar|drop_image|drop_voice|talent_image|evidence`) drives size/type limits and whether the object is private (evidence) or public-read.
